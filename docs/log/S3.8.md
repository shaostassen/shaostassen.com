# S3.8 — bits-to-messages: levels 05–07 on the instrument

Status: done · Started: 2026-08-21 · Shipped: 2026-08-21

Follow-on to S3.7, requested directly by Shao ("continue on this as far as
possible"), with an explicit carve-out: **no Smith & Nephew content**, so
nothing in S4.2 was touched.

## What shipped

The instrument went from a single frame-inspector panel to a real ladder: seven
rungs with a nav, `?level=` deep links taking either the number or the slug, and
a footer naming the four that are still undrawn. `/projects/bits-to-messages/lab`
measures **96/100/100/100** — a point *up* from S3.7 despite the page tripling
from 4.35 kB to 13.3 kB.

Levels 01–04 were split out of the old panel; 05 ERRORS, 06 FIFO and 07 FLOW are
new. Each has a failure you trigger, which is the project's own
non-negotiable — a level without one is not finished.

## Two prose defects found by checking rather than assuming

Both were in level 01 and level 03, both shipped in S3.7, and both were caught by
driving the built page and reading what actually came back.

- **Level 01 claimed a polarity mismatch is silent.** It is not. Every frame
  raises a framing error, because the stop bit is inverted along with everything
  else. Nor are the received bytes the complement of what was sent — I asserted
  that and the run returned `FA 5A` for `41 69`, where the complement would be
  `BE 96`. With the line idling at space the receiver never sees an idle level
  to wait on, triggers on the wrong edge, and reads frames straddling two real
  ones. The honest lesson is the one that survived and is now what the level
  says: framing error is *also* exactly what a baud mismatch produces, so at
  this layer the receiver can say the link is broken and has no way to say why.

- **Level 03 claimed the start bit is revalidated by a single look at tick 8.**
  It is voted two-of-three at ticks 7, 8 and 9 like every other bit, so a glitch
  has to own two of the three. Measured boundary: 8 ticks rejected, 9 accepted.
  The level now states the mechanism and the stat reads "revalidated at ticks
  7/8/9".

Neither was caught by a type or a test. Both were caught by reading the rendered
output against the claim — which is the only thing that catches this class of
defect, and the reason `content-voice`'s "every claim is backed" has to mean
backed by something executed.

## Decisions

- **`.prettierignore` gains `src/components/bits/`.** Formatting the vendored
  copy created immediate drift against the sync script, exactly as the existing
  comment for `src/components/electrons/` warns. The comment is now written for
  both rather than for electrons specifically.

- **Two more engine modules vendored, not the whole engine.** Levels 06 and 07
  need `fifo` and `flow`, so the subset is seven modules rather than five. The
  four undrawn layers (COBS, CRC, packet, ARQ) still stay out — they would be
  unused bytes on a budgeted page.

- **Level 01 stays the deterministic initial render.** The URL is read in an
  effect after mount rather than during render, so the whole first rung still
  ships in the static export and works with JavaScript disabled. Pinned by test.

- **The ladder states its own incompleteness.** "7 of 11 rungs are drawn", with
  the remaining four named. A ladder that silently started at seven would
  misrepresent both what exists and what is left, and the case study's metric
  row now leads with `rungs drawn: 7 of 11` for the same reason.

## Result

238 Playwright tests, up from 228. The five new ones pin the failures rather
than the chrome: two flipped bits passing parity undetected, a slow service
routine losing bytes off a perfect wire, XON/XOFF eating three bytes out of a
ten-byte firmware image, deep links by number and by slug, and the ladder
reporting what it has not drawn.

One of my own new assertions was too loose and failed honestly: matching the
text `/outside the budget/` also matched level 04's permanent "break it:" header,
so it would have passed for the wrong reason. It now asserts on the decoded
bytes.

`/projects` held at 95 with no new card. Full gate green.

## 2026-08-07 — SpeechLens: the confidence result

The upstream project ran the experiment its earlier write-up had listed as an
open follow-up, and the answer changed the case study's centre of gravity. The
Result section is now two parts: the measured numbers, then a narrative
section on the safety net that didn't work, which is the more interesting
half.

- `avg_logprob` turns out to be computed **once per 30-second decode window**
  and copied onto every segment from it — 22 segments, 5 distinct values on a
  130 s clip. The flag was a per-window judgement labelled per-segment.
- Per-word probability, already in the JSON and read by nothing, reaches
  **AUROC 0.89** on clean audio vs **0.58** for the segment signal, over 73
  utterances and ~1,160 words per condition.
- A published result failed to replicate: entropy-based estimators (reported
  1.5–4× better for CTC/transducer models) showed no advantage on Whisper.

Kept the failure prominent rather than smoothing it into a wins list. The
sequence — wrong threshold, then wrong granularity, then the signal that was
sitting unused — is the part that demonstrates engineering judgement, and the
copy-voice rule about honest difficulty argues for leading with it.
